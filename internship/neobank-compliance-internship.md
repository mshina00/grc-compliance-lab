## Project 1: UK Neobank Cybersecurity Research
- **Goal:** Assess the cybersecurity posture, compliance practices, and risk management of major UK neobanks and recommend improvements.
- **My work:** Reviewed public references and compliance documentation of UK neobanks such as Monzo, Revolut, Starling Bank, and Atom Bank; examined FCA and PRA guidance; analyzed GDPR and PSD2 data protection; studied authentication (MFA, biometrics, session management) and encryption (in transit and at rest); reviewed threat intelligence, NIST cybersecurity compliance guidance, and pen-testing frameworks (OWASP, NIST).
- **Key findings:** Third-party cloud reliance creates shared-responsibility risk; API access control and token handling are recurring weaknesses; SOC monitoring and threat intelligence need improvement; incident response documentation varies.
- **Recommendations:** Stronger third-party risk management, API token rotation and anomaly detection, Zero Trust principles, awareness training, and tested incident response playbooks.
- **NIST alignment:** findings mapped to NIST CSF 2.0, SP 800-53, SP 800-63B, SP 800-207, and SP 800-115 (see the report).
- **Evidence:** `internship/fortnight-report-uk-neobank-research.md`
