# Lab 06: Internal Audit and Findings Report

**Time:** 3 hours | **Frameworks:** ISO 19011 audit guidance, ISO 27001 Clause 9.2, SOC 2, IIA Standards

## Objectives
Plan a small audit, evaluate evidence, and write professional findings.

## Scenario
Audit the **user access management** process for the patient portal. Evidence provided (fictional):
- 40 leavers in the last 6 months; 6 accounts were still active after 30 days.
- Admin access reviews were done twice, but the policy requires quarterly.
- MFA is enforced for all admins but optional for support staff.

## Tasks
1. Write an audit scope, objective, and criteria (policy and framework references).
2. Create a testing approach: population, sample size, and what evidence you would request.
3. Use `templates/audit-finding-template.md` to write **three findings** from the evidence.
4. Rate each finding (High, Medium, Low) and propose remediation with owners and dates.
5. Write a short audit conclusion.

## Deliverables
Audit plan (one page) and findings report.

## Self-check
- Does each finding state condition, criteria, cause, effect, and recommendation?
- Is the rating justified by impact, not tone?
- Are findings factual and independent of blame?
