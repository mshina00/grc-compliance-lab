# Lab 04: Third-Party (Vendor) Risk Assessment

**Time:** 2 hours | **Frameworks:** ISO 27001 A.5.19-A.5.22, NIST CSF GV.SC, HIPAA BAA, GDPR Article 28, DORA

## Objectives
Tier vendors by risk, assess their controls, and decide whether to onboard.

## Scenario
Northwind wants to onboard **ChatBridge**, a fictional AI customer-support tool that would process patient messages and be hosted in a third-party cloud. It provides a SOC 2 Type I report only.

## Tasks
1. Tier the vendor (Critical, High, Medium, Low) using data sensitivity, access level, and business criticality.
2. Complete `templates/vendor-questionnaire.md` using the facts above (mark unknowns as open questions).
3. List contractual requirements: BAA, DPA, breach notice period, right to audit, subprocessor disclosure, data location, exit and data return.
4. Identify risks and add two to your Lab 01 risk register.
5. Make a recommendation: approve, approve with conditions, or reject. Justify it.

## Deliverables
Completed questionnaire and a one-page vendor risk decision memo.

## Self-check
- Why is a SOC 2 Type I weaker evidence than Type II?
- Did you address data flows to subprocessors?
- Are conditions measurable and time-bound?
