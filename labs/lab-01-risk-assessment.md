# Lab 01: Risk Assessment and Risk Register

**Time:** 2-3 hours | **Frameworks:** ISO 27005, NIST SP 800-30, ISO 27001 Clause 6.1

## Objectives
- Identify and describe risks clearly.
- Score risks with a consistent method.
- Choose treatments and assign owners.
- Calculate residual risk.

## Scenario
Northwind Health Tech hosts a patient portal and processes card payments on AWS-style cloud infrastructure. It has 150 employees, 40 vendors, and no formal risk register.

## Tasks
1. Review `artifacts/Sample_Risk_Register_GRC.xlsx` (Scoring Criteria tab first).
2. Identify **5 new risks** not already listed (ideas: ransomware, AI tool data leakage, misconfigured cloud storage, lost laptops, DDoS).
3. Write each as: *If [cause], then [event], resulting in [impact].*
4. Score inherent likelihood and impact (1-5). Justify each score in one sentence.
5. Pick a treatment (Avoid, Mitigate, Transfer, Accept) and map to a control (ISO 27001 Annex A or NIST CSF).
6. Assign an owner role, due date, and residual score.
7. Review the heat map. Which three risks need leadership attention first, and why?

## Deliverables
- Updated risk register (xlsx) with your 5 new risks.
- A half-page executive summary of the top 3 risks.

## Self-check
- Does every risk have a single accountable owner?
- Does residual risk fall below inherent risk, and is the reason stated?
- Are descriptions specific ("unpatched web server") rather than vague ("security issues")?
- Which risks are accepted, and who approved the acceptance?
