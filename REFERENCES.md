## 1. Status checks performed (6 October 2026)
These items change often, so they were checked against current reporting. Re-verify on the official pages in Section 2.

| Item | Status found | Where to confirm |
|---|---|---|
| **CMMC** | The 32 CFR 170 program rule took effect 16 Dec 2024 and the DFARS rule 10 Nov 2025. Industry reporting says the DoD suspended Phase 2 (mandatory third-party Level 2 assessments, previously 10 Nov 2026) on 13 Jul 2026 pending a review, while Phase 1 self-assessments remain in force. | DoD CIO CMMC page |
| **EU AI Act** | The AI Omnibus delayed high-risk obligations: stand-alone (Annex III) systems to 2 Dec 2027 and AI embedded in regulated products (Annex I) to 2 Aug 2028. Prohibitions (since Feb 2025) and general-purpose AI obligations (since Aug 2025) were not moved. | EUR-Lex, European Commission AI Act page |
| **CIRCIA** | No final rule yet. CISA's final rule was reported as sent to the White House (OIRA) for review in early October 2026, with publication expected before year end. Proposed requirements remain 72 hours for incidents and 24 hours for ransom payments. | CISA CIRCIA page, Federal Register |
| **ISO/IEC 27701** | The 2025 edition (published October 2025) is a standalone privacy management standard and no longer an extension of ISO 27001. | ISO |
| **NIST SP 800-61** | Revision 3 was published in 2025. | NIST CSRC |
