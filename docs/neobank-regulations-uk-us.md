# Neobank Regulatory Landscape: UK and US (Reference)

> General educational reference, not legal advice and not an employer deliverable. Verify current rules at official sources. Last reviewed: October 2026.

A **neobank** is a digital-only bank or fintech offering banking services without branches. In the US, many neobanks partner with a chartered bank; in the UK, some hold an authorisation or an e-money licence.

## United Kingdom

| Area | Name / full form | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Regulators | **FCA** (Financial Conduct Authority) | Conduct, consumer protection, financial crime oversight |
| | **PRA** (Prudential Regulation Authority, Bank of England) | Prudential safety of banks |
| | **ICO** (Information Commissioner's Office) | Data protection regulator |
| | **FOS** (Financial Ombudsman Service) | Resolves unresolved consumer complaints |
| | **PSR** (Payment Systems Regulator) | Payment systems, including fraud reimbursement rules |
| Data privacy | **UK GDPR** and **DPA 2018** (Data Protection Act 2018) | Personal data protection, data subject rights, breach reporting |
| Payments / e-money | **PSRs 2017** (Payment Services Regulations 2017); **EMRs 2011** (Electronic Money Regulations 2011) | Authorisation, safeguarding, and security for payment and e-money firms |
| Consumer | **Consumer Duty** (FCA) | Requires good outcomes for retail customers |
| Complaints | **DISP** (FCA Dispute Resolution: Complaints sourcebook) | Complaint handling and referral to FOS |
| Financial crime | **MLRs 2017** (Money Laundering Regulations 2017); **POCA 2002** | AML, customer due diligence (KYC), suspicious activity reporting |
| Governance | **SM&CR** (Senior Managers and Certification Regime) | Individual accountability of senior staff |
| Resilience | FCA/PRA **operational resilience** rules | Important business services must stay within impact tolerances |
| Outsourcing / third parties | FCA SYSC outsourcing rules; **Critical Third Parties regime** | Oversight of key service providers |
| Security standards | **PCI DSS**, **ISO 27001**, **Cyber Essentials** | Common voluntary or contractual security baselines |

## United States

| Area | Name / full form | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Regulators | **CFPB** (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau); **OCC** (Office of the Comptroller of the Currency); **FDIC** (Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation); **FinCEN** (Financial Crimes Enforcement Network); **FTC** (Federal Trade Commission); state regulators (for example NYDFS) | Consumer protection, bank supervision, AML, privacy enforcement |
| Privacy / security | **GLBA** (Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act): Privacy Rule and Safeguards Rule | Protect consumers' financial information |
| | **CCPA / CPRA** (California), other state privacy laws | Consumer privacy rights |
| | **NYDFS 23 NYCRR 500** | Cybersecurity program requirements in New York |
| Financial crime | **BSA** (Bank Secrecy Act) and USA PATRIOT Act | AML program, KYC, suspicious activity reports |
| Consumer protection | **Reg E** (Electronic Fund Transfer Act); **UDAAP** (Unfair, Deceptive, or Abusive Acts or Practices) | Error resolution, unauthorized transfers, fair treatment; complaint handling |
| Payments | **PCI DSS** | Card data security |
| Supervision | **FFIEC** (Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council) guidance | IT examination standards |

## UK vs US: Key Differences
| Topic | UK | US |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy law | One comprehensive law (UK GDPR) | Sector laws (GLBA) plus state laws |
| Regulator model | FCA / PRA / ICO | Many federal and state regulators |
| Breach notice to regulator | 72 hours (UK GDPR) | Varies by law and state |
| Complaints | FCA DISP and FOS | CFPB complaint system and company processes |
| Licensing | FCA authorisation or e-money licence | Often a partner-bank model plus state licenses |

## Data Loss Prevention (DLP) quick guide
**DLP** = Data Loss Prevention: tools and policies that detect and block unauthorized exposure of sensitive data. Typical controls: data classification, email/endpoint/cloud DLP rules, blocking uploads to unapproved services, encryption, and alerting. Relevant to UK GDPR Art. 5(1)(f) and Art. 32 (security of processing), GLBA Safeguards Rule, PCI DSS, ISO 27001 A.8.12 (data leakage prevention).
