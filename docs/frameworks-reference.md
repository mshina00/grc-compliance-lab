# Standards and Frameworks Reference

> Frameworks are voluntary unless a law, contract, or customer requires them. Verify current versions at the official sources.

| Name | Full form | Owner | Function / purpose | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** | Information Security Management Systems requirements | ISO / IEC | Certifiable standard for an Information Security Management System (ISMS) | Clauses 4-10 (context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement) plus Annex A with 93 controls in four themes: Organizational (37), People (8), Physical (14), Technological (34). Requires a Statement of Applicability (SoA). |
| **ISO/IEC 27002:2022** | Information security controls guidance | ISO / IEC | Implementation guidance for Annex A controls | Not certifiable. |
| **ISO/IEC 27005** | Information security risk management | ISO / IEC | Guidance for risk assessment under an ISMS | Pairs with ISO 31000. |
| **ISO/IEC 27701** | Privacy information management | ISO / IEC | Extends an ISMS to manage personal data (PIMS) | Maps to GDPR concepts. Check current edition. |
| **ISO 22301** | Business continuity management systems | ISO | Business continuity planning and resilience | BIA, strategies, exercising. |
| **ISO 31000** | Risk management guidelines | ISO | General enterprise risk management principles and process | Principles, framework, process. |
| **ISO/IEC 42001** | AI management systems | ISO / IEC | Governance of AI systems | Certifiable AI management standard. |
| **NIST CSF 2.0** | National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework | NIST (US) | Voluntary, outcome-based framework to manage cyber risk | Six functions: **Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover**. Organizational Profiles and Tiers. |
| **NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5** | Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations | NIST | Comprehensive control catalog for federal systems (basis for FedRAMP) | 20 control families, such as Access Control (AC), Audit and Accountability (AU), Incident Response (IR), Risk Assessment (RA). |
| **NIST SP 800-30** | Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments | NIST | Risk assessment methodology | Threat, vulnerability, likelihood, impact. |
| **NIST SP 800-37 (RMF)** | Risk Management Framework | NIST | Lifecycle for authorizing systems | Prepare, Categorize, Select, Implement, Assess, Authorize, Monitor. |
| **NIST SP 800-171** | Protecting Controlled Unclassified Information in Nonfederal Systems | NIST | Security requirements for contractors handling CUI | Basis for CMMC Level 2. Note the Rev. 2 and Rev. 3 differences. |
| **CIS Controls v8 / v8.1** | Center for Internet Security Critical Security Controls | CIS | Prioritized, practical safeguards | 18 controls with Implementation Groups IG1-IG3. |
| **COBIT 2019** | Control Objectives for Information and Related Technology | ISACA | IT governance and management framework | Governance and management objectives. |
| **SOC 2** | System and Organization Controls 2 | AICPA | Attestation report on service organization controls | Trust Services Criteria: Security (required), Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, Privacy. **Type I** = design at a point in time; **Type II** = design and operating effectiveness over a period (commonly 6-12 months). |
| **PCI DSS v4.0.1** | Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard | PCI Security Standards Council | Protects cardholder data | 12 principal requirements covering network security, secure configuration, protecting stored and transmitted account data, malware protection, secure systems and software, access control, authentication, physical access, logging and monitoring, testing, and policies. Enforced by card brands and acquirers. |
| **HITRUST CSF** | Health Information Trust Alliance Common Security Framework | HITRUST | Certifiable framework harmonizing HIPAA, ISO, NIST, and others | Common in healthcare. |
| **CSA CCM** | Cloud Security Alliance Cloud Controls Matrix | CSA | Cloud-specific control framework | Basis for the STAR program. |
| **FAIR** | Factor Analysis of Information Risk | FAIR Institute | Quantitative risk analysis in financial terms | Loss event frequency and loss magnitude. |
| **MITRE ATT&CK** | Adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Common Knowledge | MITRE | Knowledge base of attacker behavior | Useful for threat-informed risk and detection mapping. |

## The Three Lines Model (governance)
1. **First line:** operational management owns and manages risk.
2. **Second line:** risk, compliance, and security functions oversee and advise (GRC lives here).
3. **Third line:** internal audit provides independent assurance.

## Risk treatment options
Avoid, Mitigate (reduce), Transfer (insurance or contract), Accept.
