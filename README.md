# GRC & Cybersecurity Compliance Lab

A hands-on portfolio lab covering **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)** work: risk assessment, control mapping, policy writing, vendor risk, incident response, and audit.

> All companies, data, and scenarios are **fictional**. Nothing here is legal advice. Verify regulatory details against official sources before relying on them.

## Scenario
You are a GRC analyst at **Northwind Health Tech**, a fictional cloud SaaS company handling patient data and card payments. Leadership wants to prepare for **ISO 27001 certification, a SOC 2 Type II audit, and HIPAA compliance**.

## Skills demonstrated
Risk assessment and scoring, risk register management, control mapping (ISO 27001, NIST CSF, SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS), policy development, third-party risk management, incident response and breach notification analysis, internal audit and findings reporting.

## How to use
1. Read `docs/` for background.
2. Complete the labs in order. Each has objectives, tasks, deliverables, and a self-check.
3. Save your own deliverables in a `my-work/` folder (risk register, policies, findings).
4. Reference your completed work on your resume or LinkedIn.

## Suggested resume bullets
- Built an ISO 27001 / NIST CSF-aligned risk register with a 5x5 scoring method, heat map, and residual risk tracking.
- Mapped controls across ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA, and PCI DSS to identify compliance gaps.
- Drafted security policies and a vendor risk assessment process.
- Ran a tabletop incident response exercise with breach-notification timeline analysis.
